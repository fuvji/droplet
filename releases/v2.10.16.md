# Droplet 2.10.16

Droplet 2.10.16 is the DPI-proof and visual-polish release. It keeps the
mirrored 12 px layered-alpha silhouette from 2.10.15 and adds live captures at
100%, 125%, and 150% DPI with a regression check over all four corners.

## DPI regression

- `tools/capture-tabs.ps1` now exercises the live `WM_DPICHANGED` path at
  100%, 125%, and 150%. The resulting Core captures are 624×408, 780×510, and
  936×612 physical pixels.
- `tools/check-dpi-corners.ps1` reads those PNGs and requires the four outer
  corner alphas to be zero, the full corner boxes to mirror on both axes, and
  the straight edges to remain opaque. The check runs from
  `tools/check-release.ps1`.
- `src/layered.rs` exposes the same pixel-alpha definition to a unit test that
  covers all three scales. A wrong right/bottom arc centre, a square corner, or
  a DPI-dependent mask now fails the build before a release.
- A separate live desktop capture over a magenta backdrop was used during
  verification: all four corners at all three scales showed the backdrop, not
  an opaque square. This is a verification aid, not a shipped mode.

## GUI polish

The UI borrows the reference language of layered Apple surfaces without copying
brand assets or changing the product's 624×408 layout:

- A soft graphite vertical wash replaces the flat black frame, with a quieter
  rail and card surface hierarchy.
- The active rail item is a filled rounded selection instead of a left accent
  bar; the icon keeps the theme accent and the label becomes warm white.
- The panel title is larger and bold, with a small “LOCAL / WINDOWS”
  eyebrow. Card titles are bold, improving scan order without adding
  outlines or decorative glyphs.
- Sliding switches now use a white knob with a subtle shadow and a deeper
  accent track, so on/off state remains legible for the default warm-white
  theme and for custom colors.
- Sliders use a slightly deeper track and a soft halo around the white thumb.
- The glass pass was kept to one full-buffer alpha/premultiply pass. A card
  shadow path was removed after it measurably raised the System render readout;
  the final capture stays in the prior single-digit-millisecond range. No
  end-to-end speedup is claimed.

## Kept

- Keep panel open remains on by default; Reset restores that shipped default.
- The 2.10.12 held 0.5 ms timer, anti-sleep state, DPI-correct hit testing,
  pre-indexed trigger routing, and event-driven paint path are unchanged.
- No network, telemetry, game-memory access, capture evasion, anti-cheat
  bypass, input disguise, or privilege escalation was added.

## Limits

The DPI captures exercise the application's `WM_DPICHANGED` path on a 96 DPI
host; they are not a physical 125% or 150% monitor test. No live Minecraft
client is available here, so in-game hit registration remains unverified. The
input harness still cannot reliably record synthetic Shield output on this
desktop; that check remains unresolved, not a pass.
