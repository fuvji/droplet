# Droplet 2.10.0 — Hydrogen Anchor, 8 px corners, vault graph

Released 2026-11-11.

Droplet 2.10.0 ports the Anchor branch from `hydrogen.ahk`, aligns the window
corner with Windows' smaller radius, and rebuilds the site around an
Obsidian-style local vault graph.

## Anchor from Hydrogen

- The literal `A6` order is preserved: select Anchor, right-click, wait
  37–43 ms, right-click again, select Glowstone, wait 37–43 ms, tap Totem
  twice, wait the configured delay, then right-click.
- The two 37–43 ms steps are Hydrogen's fixed settles. The final settle uses
  the Min/Max sliders.
- Anchor sliders now expose Min **47–97 ms** and Max **53–103 ms**, with
  defaults **47/53**. Min and Max remain independent and are sorted only when
  the macro reads the pair.
- The **Air loop** switch restores Hydrogen's `vE` behavior. With Air on,
  holding the trigger repeats the full sequence; Toggle mode still uses the
  single latched worker.
- The three slots must still be distinct, and the busy guard still prevents a
  second press from interleaving a sequence in flight.
- No live Minecraft client was available here. The placement and detonation
  order therefore remain unverified in game.

## GUI

- `CORNER_RADIUS` is now **8 px** everywhere: paint, window region, DWM clip,
  and the capture mask. The previous 13 px region was visibly thicker than
  Windows' own corner.
- The Anchor card gains the Air loop switch. The panel remains 624×408.
- No top accent strip, painted outer hairline, module glyph, or card outline.

## Website

- The homepage now has a vault-graph hero veil and a full graph view with
  module nodes, measured links, and local-only labels.
- The graph is inline SVG and CSS. No third-party script, font, analytics, or
  network dependency was added.
- Version, downloads, captures, checksums, and release history are updated to
  2.10.0.

## Honest limits

The executable is not smaller than 2.9.3. It is native and has no runtime
dependencies, but no “instant” startup guarantee is made. Minecraft still
observes input on its own tick; the fixed Hydrogen settles and the configured
Anchor range are real waits, not a latency bypass.
