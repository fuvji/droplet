# Droplet 2.10.5 — idle watcher gates

Released 2026-11-11.

Droplet 2.10.5 removes two avoidable settings-lock acquisitions from the
background watchers. It is an internal efficiency patch, not an input-latency
or size claim.

## What changed

- `Engine` now caches whether sprint and totem are enabled alongside the
  existing focus-only cache.
- The 8 ms sprint watcher skips the settings snapshot and lock whenever the
  gate is closed or sprint is disabled. It still resets the next-pulse deadline
  so re-enabling sprint behaves exactly as before.
- The crosshair watcher skips the settings snapshot and lock when it is not
  focused or the totem module is disabled. Its 16 ms active / 100 ms idle
  cadence is unchanged.
- Active paths still read the current settings, so key bindings and module
  switches continue to update immediately on publish.
- The favicon, transparent brand mark, presence cache, and click-through
  corners from 2.10.4 are retained.
