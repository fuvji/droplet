# Droplet 2.10.8

**Windows x64 portable · 2026-11-12**

## Smaller binary, same event-gated reads

- The self-destruct recycle waiter was the only `std::process::Command` user.
  It now launches through the existing hidden `ShellExecuteW` path. The
  five-second wait and Recycle Bin deletion are retained.
- Bundled `rust-lld` plus ICF supplies the final link. The release profile still
  uses `opt-level=3`, fat LTO, one codegen unit, panic-abort, and stripped
  symbols.
- The event-gated sprint and crosshair reads introduced in 2.10.7 are
  unchanged. Polling cadence and bindings are unchanged.
- Result: **542,720 bytes**, **55,808 bytes smaller** than 2.10.7 and
  **54,272 bytes below** the 596,992-byte target.

## Measured gating

Isolated profile, hidden panel, crosshair off, 5 s warm-up, 60 s sample:

| State | Process CPU | CPU % of 16 logical CPUs | Process context switches | Sprint-thread context switches |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2.10.7 idle | 31.25 ms | 0.0033% | 25/s | 9/s |
| 2.10.7 armed | 125 ms | 0.0130% | 140/s | 126/s |
| 2.10.8 idle | 93.75 ms | 0.0098% | 24/s | 9/s |
| 2.10.8 armed | 109.375 ms | 0.0114% | 138/s | 122/s |

- Windows reports process CPU time in 15.625 ms ticks, so the CPU figures are
  short-sample approximations.
- “Armed” means `focus_only=0`, master and sprint enabled, no physical W held.
  The crosshair watcher was disabled to isolate the sprint path.
- The stable result is the gating difference: sprint-thread context switches
  move from **9/s to 126/s** on 2.10.7 and **9/s to 122/s** on 2.10.8.
- No CPU speedup, end-to-end latency speedup, zero-latency behavior, or
  render-speed claim is made.
