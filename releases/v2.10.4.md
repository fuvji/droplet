# Droplet 2.10.4 — transparent mark, leaner loop

Released 2026-11-11.

Droplet 2.10.4 is a compact cleanup release. The site brand mark now sits on
nothing, and the presence loop no longer takes the settings lock every tick.

## Transparent mark

- Removed the favicon's graphite tile. The icon is a transparent outlined
  droplet again.
- Collapsed the site brand mark to one 27 px transparent rule: no plate, border,
  background, or shadow.
- Deleted the duplicate late reset and the old plate declarations from the CSS.
  The header and footer reuse the same mark.
- The app-side header droplet was already transparent and is unchanged.

## Leaner presence loop

- Added a cached `focus_only` atomic to `Engine`, updated whenever settings are
  published.
- The 160 ms presence watcher now reads that atomic instead of cloning the full
  settings `Arc` and taking the settings lock on every tick.
- Presence, focus, click-through corners, and cold discovery are unchanged.
- The executable is **596,480 bytes**, 512 bytes smaller than 2.10.3.
