# Droplet 2.10.2 — cleaner corners, matched mark

Released 2026-11-11.

Droplet 2.10.2 fixes the hard corner edge caused by clipping the layered panel
twice. It also replaces the website’s different solid droplet with the same
outlined mark the app draws.

## Corners

- On Windows 11, the app now asks DWM for its compositor-rounded corner and
  leaves the window unclipped by GDI. That edge is antialiased and follows the
  system DPI.
- The Win32 rounded region remains as a fallback only when DWM cannot round the
  popup. The region is no longer applied on top of a working DWM clip.
- The logical radius stays **8 px**; paint, fallback region, and capture mask
  agree. The visual change is a cleaner edge, not a new radius or a thicker
  border.
- The actual compositor output was checked on a white backdrop, not only in the
  masked website captures.

## Brand mark

- The favicon, header marks, and footer marks now use the app’s original
  outlined droplet silhouette: a faint filled bowl with a controlled line.
- The favicon sits on a small graphite tile so the white outline stays visible
  in light browser chrome. The old solid droplet is gone, and the icon URL is
  cache-busted with `?v=2.10.2`.
- The site still uses only local SVG/CSS. No logo service, third-party font, or
  script was added.
