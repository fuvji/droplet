# Droplet 2.10.9

**Windows x64 portable · 2026-11-13**

## Auto Crystal Return

- Auto Crystal now has a **Return** slot (`v5S3`), default hotbar **1**, beside
  Obsidian and Crystal. It is a normal editable slot binding.
- A hold of at least **160 ms** restores Return after the trigger is released.
  The restore is a double tap of the Return binding, matching Potion and Breach.
- A latched toggle restores Return once when switched off. The request is tied
  to the worker generation, so a quick off/on cycle cannot restore in the middle
  of the next cycle.
- A short single press never qualifies. It still runs the place-and-crystal
  sequence, but deliberately leaves the current hotbar slot alone.

## DPI-correct hit testing

- The panel now handles `WM_DPICHANGED`, updating its scale, suggested physical
  window size, rounded clip, hover state, and paint invalidation together.
- Pointer coordinates stay in physical space, the same space as the scaled
  painted rectangles. At 125% and 150%, hover and clicks no longer land one row
  or one tab away from the visible control.
- The four site captures were regenerated at the canonical 624×408 size. The
  capture helpers are DPI-aware and force the panel through its DPI path so a
  high-DPI desktop cannot produce a clipped or virtualized bitmap.
- The panel's **Keep panel open** default is on, matching the legacy `vP`
  default and the first-run text shipped with the download.
