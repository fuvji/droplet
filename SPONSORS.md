# Sponsor Droplet

Droplet is funded by direct sponsorships. The product site does not load a
third-party ad client, tracking pixel, cookie banner, or analytics script.

Sponsor page: <https://fuvji.github.io/droplet/sponsors.html>

Inquiry: <https://github.com/fuvji/droplet/issues/new?title=Sponsorship%20inquiry&body=Company%3A%0APlacement%3A%0ABudget%3A%0ALink%3A>

## Inventory

| Placement | Format | Context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero partner | Mark + one line | Beneath the opening product statement |
| Benchmark partner | Label + one line | Beside the measured routing card |
| Capture partner | Label | Beside the native panel capture |
| Release partner | One line | Changelog / release notes for one version |

Each placement is static and clearly labelled. There is one partner per
placement. Rate card and availability are sent after an inquiry.

## Audience

- Minecraft Java PvP players interested in timing and one-shot sequences.
- Developers interested in Rust, Win32 input, local-first tools, and small
  binaries.
- Readers who value a product page without analytics or ad scripts.

The public site does not measure traffic, so no audience counts are claimed.

## Not accepted

- Deceptive downloads, malware, cheat loaders, account theft, or scan evasion.
- Popups, interstitials, autoplay audio, tracking pixels, or third-party
  JavaScript.
- Gambling, crypto speculation, adult content, or products that target minors.
- A placement that implies Droplet endorses a product it does not use.

## Technical boundary

The sponsor page is plain HTML/CSS. A sponsor link is ordinary outbound
navigation. Adding an ad network would require a visible change to the privacy
and security model, and is not part of 2.9.1.

## Current status

The slots are open. No sponsor revenue is live until a partner is accepted and
the static placement is filled.
