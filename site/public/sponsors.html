Policy

What we
will not run.

A sponsor is part of the page. The page has to remain useful, safe, and visibly separate from the product.

Accepted

  • Developer tools, hosting, hardware, and relevant services.
  • Static logos, short lines, and one outbound link.
  • Clear “Partner” or “Sponsored” labelling.

Not accepted

  • Deceptive downloads, malware, cheat loaders, or account theft.
  • Popups, autoplay audio, interstitials, or tracking pixels.
  • Gambling, crypto speculation, or adult content.

Technical boundary

  • No third-party JavaScript on the product page.
  • No cookies or analytics added for a placement.
  • Sponsor links are ordinary outbound navigation.
