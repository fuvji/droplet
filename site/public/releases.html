2.9.1 notes

What changed,
and what did not.

A readable ledger. Every item is implemented in the source or measured on the release build; the unverified items are labelled.

GUI

  • Text ON/OFF pills replace the round power glyphs on every module card.
  • All module glyphs are removed. Cards are one borderless surface: title left, ON/OFF pill right.
  • Dash loses the redundant “Clicks 1x” row and shrinks by one row.
  • Behaviour no longer shows a Capture row. The public audit still states ordinary visible composition.
  • Anchor ships at 37–43 ms. The separate tick-floor row is gone; the macro still enforces the 50 ms floor.
  • Deeper graphite palette, warm white text, one thin accent hairline, same 13 px corner.
  • The portable executable is 592,384 bytes — 9,216 bytes smaller than 2.9.0.

Behaviour and input

  • The tray icon and menu are gone. A manual hide is sticky across focus changes.
  • Ctrl + Right Shift shows or hides the panel.
  • Close exits immediately after the final save.
  • Self-destruct is a one-second hold with a visible percentage.
  • Dash, Shield, and Pearl select and click in one SendInput batch.
  • Unbound keys skip the macro router before cloning either Arc.

Not added

  • No streamproof or capture-evasion mode.
  • No anti-cheat bypass, process hiding, log erasure, or input disguise.
  • No zero-latency claim and no silent auto-updater.
  • No Linux or macOS package until the platform layer exists.

Ledger

Release
history.

The versioned notes in the repository are the source of truth. This page is a readable mirror, not an automatically generated feed.

2.9.1

Borderless cards, text controls

Module glyphs removed, text ON/OFF pills, borderless cards, Anchor tick-floor row retired, 592,384-byte exe and 301,774-byte MSIX.

2.9.0

Power controls and downloads

Round power buttons, transparent item glyphs, compact Dash, sticky hide, 1 s self-destruct, same-frame Dash/Shield/Pearl input, Windows ZIP and MSIX, GitHub release workflow.

2.8.0

Fitted corners and lighter paint

One 13 px radius across paint, window region, and capture mask; allocation-free binding chips; 594,944-byte binary; editorial site rebuild.

2.7.0

Accent and per-module modes

RGB presets and channel sliders, Hold/Toggle chips on every trigger card, and tighter fast-module defaults.

2.6.2

Rounded window, single mark

Window corners follow the painted radius; the brand mark becomes one transparent droplet.

Verify

Trust,
then run.

The portable exe is unsigned, so SmartScreen may warn. The MSIX is signed with a self-signed development certificate; a fresh machine must trust that leaf before sideloading. Neither package phones home.
